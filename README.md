# IDEA & School Accommodations Knowledgebase

A public, source-linked research library about **U.S. public-school accommodations**, starting with federal law and Texas. It is intended to support educators and future development of a teacher-reviewed tool that adapts **blank assignments** using a supplied list of accommodations.

**Status:** Initial research; sources and assessment policies must be checked before use. Last reviewed: **November 12, 2026**. This is educational information, not legal advice, an eligibility decision, a substitute for an individualized plan, or a guarantee of compliance.

## How to use this library

Federal statutes and regulations establish obligations; Texas statutes and adopted rules add state requirements; agency guidance helps explain implementation. Assessment rules can vary by test, administration, school year, and individual support. **A classroom accommodation is not automatically permitted on a statewide assessment.** Follow current law, agency policy, applicable district procedures, and the student's current individualized plan. If they conflict or are unclear, consult the responsible school professional or counsel.

## Public repository privacy

**Never commit student information, completed assignments, IEPs, 504 plans, individualized lists of accommodations, school records, credentials, or real case notes.** A name-free record or local student ID can still identify a person. Use invented, non-identifying examples only. Do not upload secure assessments or copyrighted assignment content without authorization.

This repository holds public reference material, not an operational student-record system. Its privacy rules do not establish that any future application is FERPA-compliant or suitable for a district's use.
