# Texas accommodations and assessment sources

Official sources checked **November 12, 2026**. Prefer the live official page or current PDF over copied excerpts. Assessment guidance is administration-specific and should be rechecked before use.

## Texas law and rules

1. **Texas Education Code, Chapter 29, Subchapter A** — special education program; includes **§29.005 (Individualized Education Program)** and current statutory notes.
   https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?chapter=ED.29&code=ED
2. **Texas Education Code §26.008** — parent information and right to request an IDEA evaluation or Section 504 aids, accommodations, or services.
   https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=ED&Value=26.008
3. **19 TAC Chapter 89, Subchapter AA (October 2026 update)** — Commissioner’s Rules Concerning Special Education Services; particularly §§89.1011, 89.1040, 89.1050, and 89.1055.
   https://tea.texas.gov/about-tea/laws-and-rules/texas-administrative-code/19-tac-chapter-89
4. **TEA 19 TAC Chapter 89 landing page** — official rule index and current subchapter links.
   https://tea.texas.gov/about-tea/laws-and-rules/texas-administrative-code/19-tac-chapter-89

## TEA assessment policy, current school year

9. **2026–2027 STAAR Accommodations Educator Guide** — current guide; defines accessibility features, locally approved designated supports, and supports requiring TEA approval; emphasizes routine and effective classroom use and practice.
   https://tea.texas.gov/data-reports/staar/staar-accommodations-educator-guide.pdf
10. **TEA Accommodations Resources / District and Campus Coordinator Resources entry point** — current policy documents, eligibility criteria, and implementation materials.
    https://tea.texas.gov/assessment-and-accountability/accommodations-resources

    https://tea2.tea.texas.gov/data-reports/student-assessment-overview/house-bill-8-student-assessment

## Use and maintenance notes

- The **2026–27** guide is the relevant STAAR accommodations baseline for the current school year, but individual policy pages and calendar deadlines control specific supports.
- The 2027–28 transition to SST means this page must be updated before describing future assessment accommodations as settled policy.
- TEA assessment pages may move or require coordinator access. If a deep link fails, start at the TEA Student Assessment or DCCR landing page and locate the current year’s document.
- This knowledgebase intentionally contains no student-identifying information.
